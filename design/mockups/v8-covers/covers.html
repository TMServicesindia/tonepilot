Song covers — A + B mixed: three options

Order of preference in the app: 1) the artwork embedded in your own MP3 / M4A (read on the iPhone, never downloaded) → 2) a generated TonePilot cover, made from the song's own analysis — mood sets the colours, sections set the shapes, the title is set in type. Pick a style below; the right column shows it on the Songs screen. Content is illustrative.
D1 · Radial AuraThe waveform wraps around the ring: bars radiate outward from the record, coloured by section; the hook glows amber with ★. Title set large above, language below.
D2 · Ring over AuraThe glowing horizontal waveform runs behind a smaller Song Ring (the record). Title top-left. The most layered and musical.
D3 · HaloA soft radial waveform halo with the section ring inside it as a thin record label; title centred under it. The calmest of the three.
